Accra Flood Susceptibility

This interactive WebGIS tool utilizes Google Earth Engine and a Multi-Criteria Evaluation (MCE) model to dynamically assess flood risk zones in Accra.

Data Parameters Included:

  • JRC Global Surface Water: Historical flood frequency analysis (2000-2024).
  • MERIT HAND: Height Above Nearest Drainage modeling for hydrological flows.
  • SRTM Elevation & Slope: Topographic risk reclassification.
  • ESA WorldCover: LULC integration focusing on built-up areas and wetlands.
